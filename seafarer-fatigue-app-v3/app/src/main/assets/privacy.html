Seafarer Fatigue AI — Privacy Policy

Research and safety-support tool. This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions.

Health Connect data

With permission, the app reads sleep sessions, resting heart rate and HRV RMSSD to support local fatigue/recovery estimation.

Storage

The app does not request Android INTERNET permission. Calculations and recent history are local. Reports leave the app only when the user explicitly exports them.

User control

Health Connect is optional and permissions can be revoked in Health Connect settings. Manual and file input remain available.

Research limitation

The fatigue model requires external validation with real seafarer observations before operational or commercial reliance. The responsible officer remains the final decision-maker.
